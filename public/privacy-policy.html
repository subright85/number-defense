Privacy Policy

Number Defense — Last updated: May 2026

Data We Collect

Number Defense does not collect or transmit personal information. Scores, nicknames, and leaderboard entries are stored locally on your device only and are never sent to any server.

Advertising

Number Defense contains no advertisements.

Children's Privacy (COPPA)

  • Number Defense is designed for all ages, including children. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).
  • We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.
  • No behavioral profiling. No targeted advertising based on user activity.
  • No account creation required.

In-App Purchases

None. Number Defense has no in-app purchases and no subscriptions.

Third-Party Services

Number Defense uses Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) to load the Inter and JetBrains Mono typefaces. Font files are fetched from Google's servers when you first load the game. No other third-party SDKs for advertising, analytics, or crash reporting are integrated.
